How this works

You'll move through a few short steps: describe what's going on, choose what would help most, look a little closer, then consider where things could go. At the end you get a plain-language summary — yours to keep, copy, or start fresh from.

There's nothing to figure out in advance. Every question is written in plain language, and you can skip anything that doesn't fit. Nothing you write leaves your browser.
