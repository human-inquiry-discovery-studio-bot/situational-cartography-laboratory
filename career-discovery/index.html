Evidence notes

What the numbers can and cannot tell you

Wages are Ontario hourly figures from Job Bank, with a 2023 to 2024 reference period. Outlooks cover 2025 to 2027 and retain Job Bank's exact vocabulary. A label such as Moderate is an official classification, not a recommendation.

Occupational groups are broad. A wage range does not guarantee an individual wage, and a provincial outlook may not describe one local employer or community.

  • Use the profile to understand the occupation.
  • Use comparison to see trade-offs without turning them into a score.
  • Use the official links to continue the research and check for later updates.
